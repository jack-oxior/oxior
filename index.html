ABOUT US

Oxior Civil Enterprises is a proudly South African, 100% black-owned construction and engineering company. Anchored by unrivalled workmanship, diligence and professionalism, we plan, build and maintain infrastructure across the civil, electrical, roads, general building, mechanical and plant-hire disciplines.


More than a contractor, we act as a strategic partner to our clients — combining competitive pricing with a relentless commitment to delivering innovative, timely and consistent results that move their objectives forward.


Since our inception we have built a reputation for reliability that turns once-off appointments into long-standing relationships. Today our management resources are fully integrated and our project teams are cross-trained across every market we serve, giving us the flexibility to resource and specialise around the unique challenges of each project.

VISION

To become and remain a reputable construction and engineering company that can compete with the best, anywhere in the world.

MISSION

To meticulously deliver work that consistently exceeds expectations — upholding the highest standards of quality, safety, leadership and performance in every commitment we make to our clients.

OUR SERVICES

Our scope of services has expanded steadily over the years, driven by a deliberate investment in quality, safety and proven ethics. We deliver as a single, accountable partner across six complementary disciplines.

01 WHAT WE DO

Civil Construction

We deliver civil engineering projects and associated works, with completed contracts across the country. Our experience spans the oil, gas and petrochemical, water and wastewater, stormwater and fire-protection sectors — each with distinct requirements that our teams meet with confidence.

02 WHAT WE DO

Plant Hire

Our modern, well-maintained earthmoving fleet is available for dry and wet plant hire to construction projects and third parties — excavators, dozers, graders, loaders, compaction equipment, trucks and crushers, supported by experienced, qualified operators.

03 WHAT WE DO

Electrical Solutions

Through our framework contracts with Eskom we build, maintain and upgrade electrical infrastructure across South Africa, using streamlined, technology-enabled workflows. Our electrical capability includes:

  • Substation construction, strengthening and upgrades
  • MV / LV overhead line construction
  • Electrification and infill projects
  • Minor and major reticulation
04 WHAT WE DO

General Building

New builds, alterations, renovations, extensions and refurbishments — we do it all. By assessing each client's individual needs we provide complete building solutions: coordinating every project aspect from supervising construction and managing trades to negotiating supplier discounts, overseeing material selection and deliveries, running daily inspections and managing budgets.

HOW WE WORK

SAFETY, HEALTH, ENVIRONMENT & QUALITY

Quality and safety are not add-ons — they are how we work. Oxior operates an integrated SHEQ management system, governed by our Governance, Risk & Compliance division and approved at top-management level. While not yet formally ISO-certified, our systems are built and maintained to align with recognised ISO standards across three disciplines.

◈

Health & Safety

An occupational health and safety system aligned to ISO 45001:2018, driven by our vision of Zero Harm — ongoing risk assessment with employee participation, incident investigation and learning, and simple, non-negotiable standards.

◆

Quality

A quality management system aligned to ISO 9001:2015 — understanding client needs, doing the right thing right the first time, and delivering optimal, cost-effective work on time.

❖

Environment

An environmental management system aligned to ISO 14001 principles and Section 28 of NEMA — pollution prevention, sustainable use of resources, protection of biodiversity, and a goal of zero environmental incidents.

Personnel at every level are held accountable for SHEQ performance, and we pursue continual improvement through clear objectives, training and regular review on every site.
